# Functional requirements

## FR-901 Open the cart
impl: UC-901.s1  prio: must

The system shall open the cart on request.

## FR-902 Show the total
impl: UC-901.s2  prio: must

The system shall show the total including VAT according to BR-901.

## FR-903 Empty cart hint
impl: UC-901.s2a  prio: should

The system shall show a hint if the cart is empty.
